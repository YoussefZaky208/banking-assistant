# Sending and receiving money

## 1. Receiving money
To receive money from another bank, share your Papyrus account details from **Account > Account details**: your name, account number and IBAN. Transfers from banks in Egypt usually arrive within minutes and at most 1 working day. Transfers from abroad take 1–5 working days. Receiving money is free; the sender's bank or intermediary banks may charge their own fees.

## 2. Money sent to me hasn't arrived
If a bank transfer to your Papyrus account hasn't appeared, check with the sender that they used the exact account number and IBAN, and ask for the transfer receipt. Local transfers can take up to 1 working day and international transfers up to 5 working days. Transfers sent after 5 pm or on Fridays, Saturdays and public holidays are processed on the next working day. If it still hasn't arrived after that, send us the receipt.

## 3. Sending money
In the app, go to **Payments > New transfer**. You can send money to:
- other Papyrus customers (instant, free),
- bank accounts in Egypt (usually instant, at most 1 working day),
- bank accounts abroad (1–5 working days).

## 4. Transfer fees
- To Papyrus customers: free
- To banks in Egypt: free up to EGP 70,000 per transfer; above that, EGP 5
- International transfers: 0.5% of the amount (minimum EGP 100)
Intermediary banks may take their own fee from international transfers, so the recipient can receive slightly less. The exact fee is always shown before you confirm.

## 5. Transfer timing
Transfers between Papyrus customers are instant. Transfers to banks in Egypt usually arrive within minutes but can take up to 1 working day. International transfers take 1–5 working days, depending on the country and intermediary banks.

## 6. Pending transfers
A transfer is **pending** while we or the receiving bank process it. Large or unusual transfers may need extra security checks, which can add up to 1 working day. You can follow the status in **Payments > Activity**.

## 7. Cancelling a transfer
Transfers to Papyrus customers are instant and cannot be cancelled. A transfer to another bank can be cancelled while its status is **pending**: tap it, then **Cancel transfer**. Scheduled transfers can be cancelled any time before the scheduled date. Once a transfer is completed, only the recipient can send the money back.

## 8. Declined and failed transfers
A transfer can be **declined** before it is sent: for example your balance is too low, you reached your daily limit (EGP 200,000), or the transfer failed our security checks. A transfer can **fail** after it is sent, usually because the recipient's details are wrong or their account is closed; the money is then returned to your balance within 2–5 working days.

## 9. Recipient says they didn't receive the money
Check the transfer status in the app. If it is completed, share the transfer receipt (**tap the transfer > Share receipt**) with the recipient so their bank can trace it. If the details were wrong and the transfer was not returned, contact us and we will try to recall it; this is not always possible.

## 10. Beneficiary not allowed
We may not allow some recipients, for example accounts in countries we don't support, accounts that our security checks flagged, or transfers where the recipient name doesn't match the account. In this case the app shows **Beneficiary not allowed**. Check the recipient's details; if you think it's a mistake, contact us.

## 11. Balance not updated after a transfer
If you made a bank transfer to top up your Papyrus account and your balance hasn't changed, the transfer may still be on its way (up to 1 working day for local banks). Check that you used your exact account number and IBAN. If more than 1 working day has passed, send us the receipt from your other bank.
