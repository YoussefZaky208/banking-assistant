# Cash and ATMs

## 1. Where can I use my card
You can withdraw cash at any ATM that shows the Visa logo, in Egypt and abroad. Your first use of a new card must be with the chip and PIN.

## 2. Cash withdrawal fees
- In Egypt: free up to EGP 5,000 per calendar month in total; after that, 2% of the amount (minimum EGP 5).
- Abroad: 2% of the amount.
Some ATM operators add their own fee; the ATM shows it on the screen before you confirm. We cannot refund fees charged by ATM operators.

## 3. Withdrawal limits and declined withdrawals
If you can't take money out at an ATM, check the limits and reasons below. You can withdraw up to EGP 20,000 per day and EGP 100,000 per month. A withdrawal can be declined if you reached these limits, your balance is too low, you entered a wrong PIN, the card is frozen, or ATM withdrawals are switched off in your security settings.

## 4. Pending cash withdrawals
A withdrawal can show as pending for up to 2 working days. If the ATM did not give you any cash, the amount is returned automatically within 5 working days. If it isn't, report it (section 6).

## 5. Exchange rate for cash abroad
When you withdraw cash abroad, some ATMs offer to convert the amount into Egyptian pounds for you ("dynamic currency conversion"). Always choose to be charged in the **local currency**: the ATM's conversion rate is usually much worse than ours. If you accepted the ATM's conversion, we cannot change that rate.

## 6. Wrong amount of cash received
If the ATM gave you less cash than the amount taken from your balance, report it within 30 days: tap the withdrawal in the app, then **Report a problem**, and tell us how much cash you received. The ATM operator checks the machine's cash count, which takes 10–15 working days. If the error is confirmed, the difference is returned to your balance.

## 7. Withdrawals you don't recognise
If you see a cash withdrawal you didn't make, freeze your card immediately and report the withdrawal in the app. Then order a new card. Do not share your PIN with anyone.
