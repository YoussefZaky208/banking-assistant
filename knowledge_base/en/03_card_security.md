# Card and account security

## 1. Lost or stolen card
Freeze your card immediately in the app: **Cards > select card > Freeze**. Freezing is instant and you can undo it if you find the card. If you are sure the card is lost or stolen, tap **Report lost or stolen**: we will cancel the card permanently and you can order a replacement (EGP 100). Check your recent transactions; if you see payments you don't recognise, report them (see the Disputes guide).

## 2. Card details compromised
If you think someone else knows your card number or has used it (for example after a phishing message or a suspicious website), freeze the card, report it as compromised in **Cards > select card > Report compromised**, and order a new card. Never share your PIN, your app passcode or the codes we send by SMS with anyone. Papyrus Bank staff will never ask you for them.

## 3. Card kept (swallowed) by an ATM
If an ATM keeps your card, freeze the card in the app right away. For security reasons the ATM operator destroys retained cards, so we cannot return it to you. Order a replacement card in the app (EGP 100). If the ATM also kept your cash or didn't give you the right amount, see the Cash and ATMs guide.

## 4. Lost or stolen phone
If you lose your phone, log in to the app from another phone: you will be logged out of the old device automatically. If you cannot log in on another device, contact our support team to block access to your app (see the Contact guide). Freeze your cards as a precaution; you can unfreeze them later.

## 5. Changing your PIN
You can change your PIN at any ATM in Egypt that shows the Visa logo, in the **PIN services** menu. You can always see your current PIN in the app under **Cards > Show PIN**.

## 6. PIN blocked
Your PIN is blocked after 3 wrong attempts. To unblock it, go to **Cards > select card > Unblock PIN** in the app, then make your next payment with the correct PIN at an ATM or chip-and-PIN terminal.

## 7. Forgotten app passcode
If you forgot the passcode for the app, tap **Forgot passcode?** on the login screen. We will send a code by SMS to your registered phone number and ask you to take a selfie to confirm it's you. You can then choose a new passcode.
