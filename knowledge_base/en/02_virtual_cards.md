# Virtual and disposable cards

## 1. Virtual cards
A virtual card is a card that exists only in the app. It has its own card number, expiry date and security code (CVV), and you can use it for online payments and with Google Pay or Apple Pay. Create one in **Cards > New virtual card**. You can have up to 5 virtual cards at the same time, and they are free.

## 2. Disposable virtual cards
A disposable virtual card is a virtual card that is used once. After each successful payment, the card is automatically destroyed and a new card number is created. This protects you when you shop on a website you don't fully trust, because the card number cannot be used again. Create one in **Cards > New disposable card**. Disposable cards are free.

## 3. Disposable card limits
- Maximum EGP 20,000 per payment
- Up to 20 disposable cards per day
- Disposable cards cannot be used for subscriptions, recurring payments or ATM withdrawals, because the number changes after each payment.

## 4. My virtual card isn't working
If a payment with your virtual card is declined, check that:
- the card is not frozen (**Cards > select card**),
- you have enough balance in the currency of the payment or in EGP,
- you entered the correct expiry date and CVV,
- the merchant accepts online Visa payments.
Some merchants do not accept virtual cards for certain services (for example hotel deposits or car rentals). If it still doesn't work, you can delete the virtual card and create a new one; this is free.
