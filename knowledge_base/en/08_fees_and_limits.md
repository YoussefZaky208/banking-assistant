# Fees and limits

## 1. Account fees
Opening and keeping a Papyrus account is free. There is no monthly fee and no minimum balance.

## 2. Card fees
- First physical card: free
- Replacement or additional physical card: EGP 100
- Virtual and disposable cards: free
- Card payments in Egyptian pounds: free
- Card payments in another currency: 1% exchange fee (see section 4)

## 3. Cash withdrawal fees
- ATMs in Egypt: free up to EGP 5,000 per month, then 2% (minimum EGP 5)
- ATMs abroad: 2%
- ATM operators may add their own fee.

## 4. Exchange fees
- Card payments and ATM withdrawals in another currency: Visa exchange rate + 1%.
- Exchanging money in the app: free up to EGP 50,000 per calendar month, then 0.5%.

## 5. Top-up fees
- Debit card: free · Credit card: 1.5% · Bank transfer: free · Cash at a partner point: EGP 10

## 6. Transfer fees
- To Papyrus customers: free
- To banks in Egypt: free up to EGP 70,000 per transfer, then EGP 5
- International: 0.5% (minimum EGP 100), plus possible intermediary bank fees

## 7. Main limits
- ATM withdrawals: EGP 20,000 per day, EGP 100,000 per month
- Transfers: EGP 200,000 per day
- Top-ups: EGP 500,000 per 30 days
- Maximum balance: EGP 1,000,000
- Disposable card payment: EGP 20,000

## 8. Why was I charged a fee?
Every fee appears as its own line in your activity, with a short description. Tap it to see what it was for. If you think a fee was charged by mistake, contact us within 60 days.
