# Currencies and exchange

## 1. Currencies you can hold
Your account can hold Egyptian pounds (EGP), US dollars (USD), euros (EUR), British pounds (GBP), Saudi riyals (SAR) and UAE dirhams (AED). You can pay with your card in more than 150 currencies. We do not support cryptocurrencies.

## 2. Exchanging money in the app
Go to **Account > Exchange**, choose the currencies and the amount. You see the exact rate and fee before you confirm, and the exchange happens instantly. Exchanging is free up to EGP 50,000 (or the equivalent) per calendar month, then 0.5%.

## 3. Exchange rates
In the app, we use a real-time rate based on the market rate, shown before you confirm. Rates change during the day. Outside market hours (from Friday evening to Sunday morning), rates may move less often and we may add a small margin, which is always shown before you confirm.

## 4. Card payments in another currency
When you pay in a currency you hold (for example USD), we take the money from that currency with no fee. Otherwise, we convert from EGP using the Visa exchange rate on the day the payment is completed, plus our 1% fee. The rate on the day the payment was pending can differ from the final rate, so the final amount can change slightly. If a merchant abroad offers to charge you in EGP, choose the local currency instead: their rate is usually worse.

## 5. Countries we support
Papyrus accounts are for residents of Egypt who are 18 or older. You can use your card abroad in any country where Visa is accepted, except countries under international sanctions. You can send money to most countries; the app tells you if a country is not supported.
