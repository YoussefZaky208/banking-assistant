# Contacting support

## 1. How to reach us
- In the app: **Help > Chat with us**, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- By phone: the number on the back of your card (demo number: +20 2 0000 0000).
Our assistant answers first. You can ask to talk to a person at any time, and you will be transferred to a member of our team.

## 2. Urgent problems
If your card or phone is lost or stolen, or you see payments you didn't make, freeze your card in the app first, then contact us. Freezing works instantly, even outside working hours.

## 3. Complaints
If you're not happy with our service, tell us in the chat and choose **Make a complaint**. We reply within 15 working days.

## 4. Staying safe
Papyrus Bank will never ask for your PIN, your full card number, your app passcode or the codes we send you by SMS. If someone asks for them, even if they say they work for us, it is a scam. Report it in the app.
