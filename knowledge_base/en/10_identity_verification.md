# Identity verification

## 1. Why we verify your identity
Like every bank, we are required by law to know who our customers are. This protects you and helps prevent fraud and money laundering. You need to verify your identity before you can order a card, send money or top up more than EGP 5,000.

## 2. How to verify your identity
In the app, go to **Profile > Verify identity**. You will need:
- your Egyptian national ID card, or your passport if you are a foreign resident,
- a selfie, taken in the app.
Take the photos in good light, with all four corners of the document visible. Most verifications are done within minutes; some take up to 24 hours.

## 3. I can't verify my identity
Verification can fail if the photo is blurry or has reflections, the document is expired, the name or date of birth doesn't match what you entered, or the selfie doesn't match the photo on the document. Try again in better light. After 3 failed attempts, our team reviews your documents manually within 2 working days. If you need help, contact us.

## 4. Age requirement
You must be at least 18 years old to open a Papyrus account.

## 5. Source of funds
For large amounts (for example top-ups above EGP 500,000 within 30 days, or unusual transfers), we may ask where the money comes from. This is a legal requirement. You can upload documents such as a salary slip, a bank statement from the last 3 months, a sale contract or an inheritance document in **Profile > Documents**. We review them within 2 working days. Until then, some features may be limited.
