# Cards – ordering, delivery and activation

## 1. Your Papyrus card
Every Papyrus Bank account comes with a Visa debit card. We do not issue Mastercard cards. Your card works anywhere Visa is accepted, in Egypt and abroad, in shops, online and at ATMs. There are few restrictions on where you can use it: Some merchants (for example certain car rental companies and hotels) only accept credit cards; they may refuse a debit card even though it is a Visa card.

## 2. Ordering a physical card
You can order a physical card in the app: **Cards > Order physical card**. Your first physical card is free. A replacement card (lost, stolen, damaged or swallowed by an ATM) costs EGP 100. You need a verified identity and a delivery address in Egypt before you can order a physical card.

## 3. Delivery times
Cards are delivered by courier:
- Cairo and Giza: 3–5 working days
- Other governorates: 5–7 working days
We do not deliver cards outside Egypt. You can follow the delivery status in the app under **Cards > Track delivery**, and you will receive an SMS when the courier is on the way.

## 4. My card hasn't arrived
If your card has not arrived 10 working days after you ordered it, open **Cards > Track delivery** and tap **Report a problem**. We will cancel the old card (so nobody else can use it) and send a new one free of charge. Until it arrives, you can pay online with a virtual card (see the Virtual cards guide).

## 5. Activating your card
When your card arrives, activate it in the app: **Cards > Activate card**, then enter the last 4 digits printed on the card. Your PIN is shown in the app under **Cards > Show PIN**. Your first payment with a new card must be a chip-and-PIN payment or an ATM withdrawal; contactless works after that.

## 6. Additional (spare) cards
You can have up to 3 physical cards on one account, for example a spare card to keep at home. Each additional physical card costs EGP 100. All cards share the same balance.

## 7. Card expiry
Your card is valid until the date printed on it. About 30 days before it expires, we automatically send you a new card to the address in your profile. Check that your address is correct in **Profile > Personal details**. If your card expires and you have not received a new one, order one in the app; it is free in this case.

## 8. Linking cards
To link a new physical card to your account, activate it (section 5). You can also link a debit card from another Egyptian bank to top up your Papyrus account: **Top up > Add card**. See the Top-ups guide for details.
