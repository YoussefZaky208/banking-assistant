# Adding money (top-ups)

## 1. Ways to add money
You can add money to your Papyrus account in three ways:
- with a debit or credit card issued in Egypt (instant),
- by bank transfer to your Papyrus account number (see the Transfers guide),
- with cash at a partner payment point (shops showing the Papyrus logo).
We do not accept cheques.

## 2. Supported cards for top-ups
You can top up with Visa, Mastercard and local debit cards issued by banks in Egypt. Cards issued outside Egypt, prepaid gift cards and business cards are not accepted. You can top up in Egyptian pounds (EGP); money in other currencies is added by exchanging in the app.

## 3. Top-up fees
- Debit card: free
- Credit card: 1.5% (your card issuer may also treat it as a cash advance)
- Bank transfer: free
- Cash at a partner payment point: EGP 10 per top-up

## 4. Top-up limits
- Minimum top-up: EGP 100
- Maximum per card top-up: EGP 50,000
- Maximum total top-ups: EGP 500,000 per 30 days
- Maximum balance: EGP 1,000,000
Higher limits may be possible after a source of funds check (see the Identity guide).

## 5. Verifying a card for top-ups
The first time you top up with a new card, we may ask you to verify it. We make a small temporary charge (less than EGP 10) with a 4-digit code in the description. Find the code in your other bank's app or statement and enter it in **Top up > Verify card**. The temporary charge is returned within a few days.

## 6. Pending top-ups
Card top-ups are usually instant but can take up to 30 minutes. Bank transfer top-ups can take up to 1 working day. Cash top-ups appear within 2 hours. If a cash top-up hasn't appeared after 2 hours, keep the receipt from the payment point and contact us.

## 7. Failed top-ups
A card top-up can fail because the card is not supported, the card is not verified, your other bank declined the payment (for example for online payments or insufficient funds), or the top-up would go over your limits. Try another card, or contact the bank that issued the card.

## 8. Reverted top-ups
Sometimes a top-up is added and then reversed. This happens when the other bank cancels the payment, or when the name on the card does not match your Papyrus account and the card fails our security checks. The money goes back to the card you used, usually within 5 working days.

## 9. Automatic top-up
With automatic top-up, we add money from your linked debit card when your balance drops below an amount you choose. Set it up in **Top up > Automatic top-up**: choose the minimum balance (for example EGP 500) and the amount to add (for example EGP 2,000). You can turn it off at any time.
