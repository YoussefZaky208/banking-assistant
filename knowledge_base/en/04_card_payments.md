# Card payments, refunds and disputes

## 1. Card not working
If your card isn't working, check in the app that the card is activated and not frozen, that it has not expired, and that you have enough balance. Make sure online payments, ATM withdrawals or payments abroad are switched on in **Cards > select card > Security settings**. If the chip is damaged, order a replacement card.

## 2. Declined card payments
A card payment can be declined because:
- your balance is too low,
- the card is frozen, expired or not yet activated,
- the payment type is switched off in your security settings,
- you entered a wrong PIN, CVV or expiry date,
- our fraud checks blocked a suspicious payment (you will get a notification asking you to confirm it was you),
- the merchant is in a country or category we don't support.
The reason is usually shown in the app next to the declined payment.

## 3. Contactless payments
Contactless works after your first chip-and-PIN payment with a new card. In Egypt, a single contactless payment can be up to EGP 600 without a PIN; above that, the terminal will ask you to insert the card and enter your PIN. Some terminals ask for the PIN from time to time for security, even for small amounts.

## 4. Google Pay and Apple Pay
You can add your Papyrus cards (physical and virtual) to Google Pay and Apple Pay from the app: **Cards > select card > Add to wallet**. Disposable cards cannot be added.

## 5. Pending card payments
When you pay, the merchant first reserves the amount; it shows as **pending**. The payment is usually completed by the merchant within 1–3 days, and at most 7 days. Hotels, car rentals and petrol stations sometimes reserve a larger amount than the final price; the difference is released when the payment is completed.

## 6. Reverted payments
If a merchant does not complete a pending payment, it is **reverted**: the money goes back to your balance automatically, usually within 7 days. You don't need to do anything.

## 7. Charged twice
If you see the same payment twice, one of them is often a pending reservation that will disappear within 7 days. If both payments are completed (not pending), contact the merchant first and ask them to refund one. If they don't, you can dispute it in the app (section 10).

## 8. Refunds
To get a refund for a purchase, ask the merchant; only the merchant can start a refund. Once the merchant sends it, a refund usually appears in your balance within 5–10 working days. If the merchant says they refunded you more than 10 working days ago and you still don't see it, ask them for the refund reference (ARN) and contact us.

## 9. Payments you don't recognise
If you see a card payment you don't recognise, first check the merchant name and date: merchants sometimes use a different company name, and family members may have used your card details. If you still don't recognise it, freeze your card and tap the payment, then **Report this payment**.

## 10. Disputes
You can dispute a card payment within 60 days of the payment date: tap the payment, then **Report a problem**. We will ask you a few questions and may ask for evidence (for example emails with the merchant). Investigations can take up to 45 days. If the dispute is accepted, the money is returned to your balance.

## 11. Direct debits
A direct debit is a payment that a company (for example a gym or an insurance company) collects from your account with your permission. If you see a direct debit you don't recognise, check whether you signed up for a service with that company. To stop a direct debit, cancel it with the company and in the app under **Payments > Direct debits**. If a direct debit was taken without your permission, report it in the app and we will investigate.

## 12. Unexpected charges on your statement
An amount on your statement that you didn't expect is usually one of: a foreign exchange fee for paying in another currency, an ATM fee, a fee charged by the ATM operator, a card replacement fee, or a pending reservation by a hotel or petrol station. Tap the transaction in the app to see the details. See the Fees guide for all our fees.
