# Managing your account

## 1. Updating your personal details
You can change your email address, phone number and home address in **Profile > Personal details**. To change your name or date of birth, upload an official document showing the new details (for example your new national ID); our team checks it within 2 working days.

## 2. Closing your account
You can close your account at any time, for free, in **Profile > Close account**. Before closing:
- move your remaining balance to another bank account,
- wait for pending payments and transfers to complete,
- cancel direct debits and subscriptions linked to your cards.
You cannot close an account with a negative balance or an open dispute. After closing, your cards stop working immediately. We keep your records for the period required by law.

## 3. Who can open an account
You can open an account if you are 18 or older and live in Egypt. You need a smartphone, a phone number registered in Egypt, and a valid national ID or passport.
